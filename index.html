What is distance running?

Distance running is the side of the sport built on endurance rather than pure speed. Sprinters like Usain bolt race for a few seconds, while distance runners like me compete in events that can last anywhere from a few minutes to several hours.

Why should you run?

It's healthy for you! Running is one of the simplest ways to take care of both your body and your mind. All you need is a pair of shoes and a little bit fo time, and you can start right outside your front door. Running strengthens your heart and lungs, builds endurance, and gives you more energy for everything else in your day.

What do I love about running?

Running is an escape from school and life stress. I always look forward to 6:30 AM morning practices and being able to run some miles with my teammates. No matter how many exams or assignments are piling up, those early runs give me time to clear my head and focus on nothing but the path in front of me.

Common race distances

Distance runners compete on the track, on cross country courses, and on the roads. These are some of the most popular events:

Popular distance running events
Event Distance Where it's raced
Mile 1,609 meters Track
Steeplechase 3,000 meters with barriers and a water pit Outdoor track
5K 5,000 meters (3.1 miles) Track and road
10K 10,000 meters (6.2 miles) Track and road
Cross country Usually 8K for college men and 6K for college women Grass and dirt courses
Marathon 42.2 kilometers (26.2 miles) Road

Where to learn (even) more

Want to keep going? These are some of my favorite places to follow the sport and learn about training.

  • WashU Athletics: schedules, rosters, and results for our cross country and track teams.
  • USA Track & Field: the national governing body for track, cross country, and road running in the US.
  • WashU XC TF Instagram Page: be up to date with our events and accomplishments!
  • Strava: an app for logging your runs and following friends and teammates.
